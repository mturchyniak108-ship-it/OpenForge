# Contributing

Contributions are welcome.

Please:

1. Keep changes focused.
2. Add or update tests when appropriate.
3. Preserve existing copyright and license notices.
4. Do not commit secrets or credentials.
5. Clearly identify third-party code.
6. Document security-sensitive changes.
7. Keep experimental components clearly identified as experimental.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution may be distributed under
the applicable license of the component being modified.
